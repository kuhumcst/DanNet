OEWN label extension for DanNet {version}
=========================================
DanNet-style RDF labels for the Open English Wordnet (OEWN), generated
from the OEWN {oewn-version} data by the DanNet project:
https://wordnet.dk/dannet

This dataset is a derivative of the Open English Wordnet
(https://en-word.net/), © the Open English Wordnet contributors, licensed
under CC BY 4.0. Open English Wordnet is a fork of Princeton WordNet
(https://wordnet.princeton.edu/); see the OEWN licence file for the
Princeton WordNet licence notice:
https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/blob/main/LICENSE.md

Changes relative to OEWN: none of the OEWN content is modified; this
dataset only adds rdfs:label triples in DanNet's label style.

This extension is likewise published under CC BY 4.0 (see LICENSE or

  WordNet for English. Proceedings of the 10th Global WordNet Conference.

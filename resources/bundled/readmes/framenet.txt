FrameNet integration for DanNet {version}
=========================================
An RDF representation of the FrameNet 1.7 frame inventory: the frames, their
frame elements and semantic types, and the semantic frame-to-frame relations,
converted from the PreMOn rendition of FrameNet ({premon-version}). It is the
target of the frame links carried by the COR.SEM senses.

Based on Berkeley FrameNet 1.7 (https://framenet.icsi.berkeley.edu) as
published by PreMOn (https://premon.fbk.eu), with the RDF representation by
Center for Sprogteknologi (CST), University of Copenhagen.

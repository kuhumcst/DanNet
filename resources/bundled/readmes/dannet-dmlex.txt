DanNet {version} (DMLex)
========================
The Danish WordNet — https://wordnet.dk/dannet

This export contains DanNet as one DMLex 1.0 (OASIS) resource, in a
Danish and an English variant. Both variants hold the same entries, as
XML and as JSON. The variants differ in the language of the tag
descriptions and of the subject-domain labels. The English variant
also has the English definitions of the interlingual concepts (CILI).
The export combines data from five source datasets:

  * DanNet — CC BY-SA 4.0
    Copyright © Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (DSL) and
    Center for Sprogteknologi (CST), University of Copenhagen.

  * DDS (Det Danske Sentimentleksikon / The Danish Sentiment Lexicon)
    — CC BY-SA 4.0, published by DSL and CST:
    https://github.com/dsldk/danish-sentiment-lexicon

  * COR (Det Centrale Ordregister / The Central Word Registry)
    — the CC0-licensed resources COR(1) {cor-version} and COR.EXT
    {cor-ext-version} published by Dansk Sprognævn (DSN):
    https://ordregister.dk

  * COR.SEM (the COR sense inventory {cor-sem-version}), CC0-licensed,
    compiled by DSL and published by DSN: https://ordregister.dk
    Supplies the official sense ids, the FrameNet frames, the polysemy
    patterns, the simple ontological types and the centrality marks.

  * OEWN (the Open English Wordnet) — CC BY 4.0, © the Open English
    Wordnet contributors: https://en-word.net
    Supplies the English sense equivalents.

The combined dataset includes CC BY-SA data. Therefore the license of
the dataset is CC BY-SA 4.0 (see LICENSE or
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The export includes two companion files, because DMLex 1.0 has no slot
for their content:

  * metadata.json — the dataset metadata, in Dublin Core terms.

  * presentation.json — the display preferences of DanNet: the order,
    the names and the grouping of the label types and the relation
    roles, in Danish and in English. The DMLex browser
    (https://github.com/kuhumcst/DMLex-browser) reads this file next
    to the JSON file. Other consumers do not need it.

How to read the data
--------------------
DMLex has no synset object. The conventions below make the export a
full wordnet. The labelTags inventory in the file explains each tag.

  * A sense label that starts with "synset-" names the DanNet synset
    of the sense. Senses with the same synset label are synonyms and
    share a definition. The labelTag of a synset gives a readable
    description, with the DanNet URI and the ILI URI as sameAs.

  * The relations array at the top level of the file holds the
    semantic relations. Each member ref is a sense id. A relation
    connects two synsets and lists every sense of the two synsets as
    members. A low obverseListingOrder on a member means a central
    synset.

  * A sense label that repeats the id of its own sense marks a sense
    with a source. The labelTag with that tag gives the deep link to
    the sense in Den Danske Ordbog (DDO) as its sameAs. An entry label
    that repeats the entry id does the same for the entry.

  * A label with a prefix is a tag from one of four inventories:
    "dnt:" marks the ontological types, "cor.sem:" the simple
    ontological types, "frame:" the FrameNet frames, and "dnp:" the
    polysemy patterns. The description of each labelTag gives the
    readable name.

  * In the English variant, a definition with the definitionType "ili"
    is the English definition of the interlingual concept. It is not a
    DanNet definition.

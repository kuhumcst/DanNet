DanNet {version} (DMLex)
========================
The Danish WordNet — https://wordnet.dk/dannet

A DMLex 1.0 (OASIS) representation of DanNet, available as both XML and
JSON. It combines data from four source datasets:

  * DanNet — CC BY-SA 4.0
    Copyright © Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (DSL) and
    Center for Sprogteknologi (CST), University of Copenhagen.

  * DDS (Det Danske Sentimentleksikon / The Danish Sentiment Lexicon)
    — CC BY-SA 4.0, published by DSL and CST:
    https://github.com/dsldk/danish-sentiment-lexicon

  * COR (Det Centrale Ordregister / The Central Word Registry)
    — the CC0-licensed resources COR(1) and COR.EXT published by
    Dansk Sprognævn (DSN): https://ordregister.dk

  * OEWN (the Open English Wordnet) — CC BY 4.0, © the Open English
    Wordnet contributors: https://en-word.net
    Provides the English sense equivalents, which are included in the
    JSON serialization only.

The data has been transformed and merged into a single DMLex resource.
As a derivative of CC BY-SA-licensed data, the combined dataset is
published under CC BY-SA 4.0 (see LICENSE or
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Two companion files accompany the DMLex data, since DMLex 1.0 has no
slot for either:

  * metadata.json — the dataset metadata in Dublin Core terms.

  * presentation.json — DanNet's own display taste. The file gives the
    order and the Danish names of the label types. It also gives the
    grouping of the relation types and the Danish names of the relation
    roles. The DMLex viewer (https://github.com/kuhumcst/dmlex-viewer)
    reads it next to the JSON. Other consumers can ignore it.

When using DanNet, please cite:

  Pedersen, Bolette S., Sanni Nimb, Jørg Asmussen, Nicolai H. Sørensen,
  Lars Trap-Jensen & Henrik Lorentzen (2009). DanNet – the challenge of
  compiling a WordNet for Danish by reusing a monolingual dictionary.
  Language Resources & Evaluation 43:269–299.

COR.SEM integration for DanNet {version}
========================================
An RDF representation of COR.SEM, the manually curated sense inventory for
COR (Det Centrale Ordregister / The Central Word Registry): one sense per
COR.SEM ID, linked to COR words and to DanNet synsets, and carrying FrameNet
frames, sentiment polarity, ontological types, systematic polysemy patterns,
topic domains, hypernym anchors, usage restrictions and centrality markings.

Based on the CC0-licensed resource COR.SEM {cor-sem-version} published by
Dansk Sprognævn (DSN): https://ordregister.dk — compiled by Det Danske
Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (DSL), with the RDF representation by Center for
Sprogteknologi (CST), University of Copenhagen.

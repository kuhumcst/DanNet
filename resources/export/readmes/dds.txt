DDS integration for DanNet {version}
====================================
An RDF conversion of DDS (Det Danske Sentimentleksikon / The Danish
Sentiment Lexicon), attaching polarity values to DanNet words and senses
using the MARL vocabulary.

Source dataset: https://github.com/dsldk/danish-sentiment-lexicon
compiled by Sanni Nimb, Sussi Olsen & Thomas Troelsgård and published by
Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (DSL) and Center for Sprogteknologi
(CST), University of Copenhagen.

The source dataset is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; this RDF conversion is
a derivative work published under the same licence (see LICENSE or
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes relative to the source: the tabular data has been converted to RDF
and linked to DanNet resources.

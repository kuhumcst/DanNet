COR integration for DanNet {version}
====================================
An RDF representation of COR (Det Centrale Ordregister / The Central Word
Registry), linking COR IDs to DanNet words and senses.

Based on the CC0-licensed COR resources COR(1) and COR.EXT published by
Dansk Sprognævn (DSN): https://ordregister.dk — with sense links provided
by Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (DSL) and Center for
Sprogteknologi (CST), University of Copenhagen.
